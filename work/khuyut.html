the story

A bilingual gift store for an embroidery atelier in Ajman: the buyer sees the name stitched before paying, the owner alone sees the margin, and the running stitch is the whole identity.

E-commerce AR · EN Back office · 3 roles UAE VAT Identity NDA

about the client

A baby-and-mother wear atelier in Ajman that embroiders names on newborn gifts and sells through Instagram. The brief: a bilingual store where the buyer sees the name stitched before paying, and a back office where the owner sees the margins and the tailor and the runner do not. The name is withheld under NDA; Khuyut stands in.

the challenge

  1. 01

    The name is the product: a live embroidery preview, in the thread colour and the calligraphic face the buyer picks, on the garment before checkout.

  2. 02

    One database, two faces: a storefront in dirhams with VAT-inclusive prices, and a back office with three roles and seven order states.

  3. 03

    A brand with no assets yet: the identity had to come from the craft itself, the running stitch.

the approach

A concept built as designed screens: the storefront on the phone (home, product, checkout) and the back office on the desktop, one dataset across all four, and the stitch as the system: dividers, step tracks, status tracks, focus rings.

my role

Research, requirements, pricing, identity and the concept screens, alone; the proposal written in Arabic.
