the story

Twenty-nine years of accounting software, ten editions of one Windows ERP, and an old static site: the brief was a modern site its owner changes from a dashboard, that brings in leads.

Website + CMS Next.js · Convex Settings as data Live Client round Egypt

about the client

An Egyptian accounting-software house, in business since 1997, with a Windows ERP in ten sector editions and an old static site. The brief: a modern site the company can change entirely from a dashboard, without a deploy, that brings in leads. Built in April, reworked with the client in August, live since.

the challenge

  1. 01

    Everything editable: copy with live counters, images, nine colours with a derived ramp, ten products with galleries, posts, downloads, all from one dashboard.

  2. 02

    A brochure of a desktop product: the old Windows screens had to live inside a modern page without looking like screenshots.

  3. 03

    A client round of twenty-eight changes in four days, the hero six times, each one measured in the browser before it was ticked.

the approach

Next.js on Convex with the site's settings as data: nine colour tokens the dashboard writes into the page, a hero whose counters are tokens, sections drawn in markup with the old screens redrawn, three tones with a wedge between them, and a runbook for the deploy.

my role

Product owner, designer and the only engineer: the PRD, the site, the CMS, the deploy, and the client round.

Ten editions
