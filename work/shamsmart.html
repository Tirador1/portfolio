the story

An ERP built around how Syrian shops trade: a register that finishes the sale when the power cuts, and a ledger that has it before the line returns.

Java · Spring Boot Electron · React SQLite · PostgreSQL Offline-first POS · ERP Dealer licensing

about the product

ShamSmart is a ground-up ERP for Syrian shops and wholesalers: a Windows desktop app on a Spring Boot core, running on SQLite for one machine or PostgreSQL across a shop's network, eight modules around one platform, sold through dealers and owned outright.

the challenge

  1. 01

    Two or three hours of state power a day and some of the slowest internet in the world: the register has to work with neither.

  2. 02

    One codebase, two databases: the same core alone on SQLite and shared on PostgreSQL, syncing between branches whenever a line exists.

  3. 03

    The market from the inside out: Syrian pounds beside dollars, checks and promissory notes, the way shops actually trade.

the approach

A modular monolith with one JPA layer and dialect-aware migrations; every write lands in the local database first and syncs later; the register is keyboard-first and the back office is a ledger.

my role

Founder, designer and the only engineer: the product, the brand, the platform, the POS, the dealer licensing portal and the marketing site, in fifteen weeks.
